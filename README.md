# NurAl-Amal (نور العمل) — 30 Days of Quranic Good Deeds & Habit Planner

> *"And whoever does righteous deeds while being a believer - he will have no fear of injustice or deprivation."* — Surah Ta-Ha (20:112)

**NurAl-Amal** is an interactive, colorful web application and spiritual habit planner designed to help you cultivate 30 transformative daily deeds drawn directly from the Holy Quran. It provides an intuitive, four-tier curriculum: **Monthly Plan**, **Weekly Milestones**, **Daily Focus Mode**, and a **Yearly 365-Day Consistency Heatmap & 12-Month Virtues Roadmap**.

---

## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 1. 📖 Authentic 30-Day Quranic Curriculum
- **30 Verified Quranic Deeds**: Each day features authentic Arabic text with diacritics (Tashkeel), Surah citation, clear English translation, practical micro-tasks, and spiritual wisdom/Tafsir insight.
- **8 Spiritual Categories**:
  - 🗣️ **Speech & Character** (*Husn al-Kalam*)
  - 👨‍👩‍👧 **Family & Kin** (*Birr al-Walidayn & Silat ar-Rahim*)
  - 🤲 **Charity & Giving** (*Sadaqah & Infaq*)
  - 🕊️ **Patience & Forgiveness** (*Sabr & 'Afw*)
  - ⚖️ **Justice & Integrity** (*'Adl & Amanah*)
  - 🤝 **Mercy & Community** (*Rahmah & Ta'awun*)
  - 📚 **Seeking Knowledge** (*'Ilm Nafi'*)
  - 📿 **Worship & Dhikr** (*Dhikrullah & Shukr*)

### 2. 🎨 Vibrant, Interactive UI & Themes
- **4 Custom Themes**:
  - 🌿 *Emerald Oasis* (Soothing green & gold)
  - 💎 *Royal Sapphire* (Majestic blue & amber)
  - 🔮 *Velvet Amethyst* (Royal violet & rose)
  - 🌅 *Desert Sunset* (Warm terracotta & golden amber)
- **Dark & Light Mode**: Smooth transitions with preserved user preference.
- **Glassmorphism & Ornate Accents**: Modern backdrop blur filters, glowing borders, and classic Arabic typography (`Amiri` and `Scheherazade New`).
- **Celebration Particle Canvas**: Dynamic fireworks of stars, crescent moons (🌙), and sparkles when completing deeds.
- **Harmonic Audio Chimes**: Web Audio API sound synthesis (no external broken audio files) for calm reflection chimes and tactile Dhikr clicks.

### 3. 🗓️ Four Multi-Scale Planning Views
1. **📅 Monthly Plan (30-Day Grid)**:
   - Interactive 30-day cards with completion checkmarks, search by keyword/Surah, and instant category filters.
   - Quick "Focus", "Info", and "Edit" buttons on every card.
2. **📆 Weekly Plan (Milestones & Habit Board)**:
   - 4 Thematic Weeks + Celebration Days 29–30.
   - Weekly completion ratio and weekly spiritual reflection notebook.
3. **☀️ Daily Focus Mode (Immersive View)**:
   - Deep-dive into today's deed with large Arabic calligraphy, translation, wisdom breakdown, and 3 checkable micro-actions.
   - **Built-in Digital Tasbih (Dhikr Companion)** with click sound, tap-to-count, Spacebar shortcut, and target milestones (33, 100, free flow).
   - Personal daily journal reflection with instant autosave.
4. **🌌 Yearly Virtues & 365-Day Activity Heatmap**:
   - GitHub-style 365-day habit tracker matrix showing your spiritual consistency.
   - The **12-Month Quranic Virtues Roadmap** anchoring every month of the year with a foundational Quranic theme.

### 4. ✏️ Fully Customizable & Editable
- **Edit Any Deed**: Modify titles, categories, Arabic verses, translations, or insights.
- **Daily Reminders & Alarms**: Set custom daily reminder times (e.g., Morning speech check, Midday charity, Evening prayer).
- **Browser Push Notifications**: Optional browser notification alerts to keep you consistent throughout your day.
- **Export & Import**: Download a complete JSON backup of your progress and notes, or import previous backups.
- **Print / PDF Ready**: Dedicated print stylesheet (`@media print`) for clean offline study.

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave).
2. No build tools, NPM packages, or backend servers required — 100% client-side, offline, and private.

```bash
# Optional: Serve with a local HTTP server
npx serve .
# or
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

---

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Spacebar**: Count on the Digital Tasbih when in Daily Focus Mode.
- **Esc**: Dismiss any open modal dialog (Native `<dialog closedby="any">`).

---

## 🛡️ Privacy & Offline Guarantee
All your completion progress, streaks, daily reflections, and customized reminders are saved locally in your browser's `localStorage`. No personal data leaves your device.
